How We Recruit

Start with the role. Recruit to the details.

Before presenting a candidate, we check clinical experience, assignment fit, travel plans, and availability.

01

Clinical fit

Specialty, unit, modality, technical background, and relevant experience.

02

Shift & assignment fit

Shift, call expectations, assignment length, and schedule preferences.

03

Location & travel

The work location and the clinician’s ability to travel for the assignment.

04

Availability

The proposed start date and the clinician’s availability for the full assignment.

Clinical Readiness

Ready for the first shift.

Once a clinician is selected, Medlivo coordinates the steps required to get the assignment ready to start.

01

Professional standing

Licensure, certifications, and role-specific eligibility confirmed for the opening.

02

Clinical verification

Experience, skills, and work history verified against the role.

03

Compliance clearance

Health, screening, background, and client- or program-specific standards completed.

04

First-shift readiness

Orientation, schedule, reporting instructions, and final start details confirmed.
