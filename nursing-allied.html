Recruiting Approach

Built to move quickly without losing the details that matter.

Nursing and allied staffing often combines speed with highly specific clinical requirements. Our recruiting approach is designed to address both.

Division Priority Match the clinician to the actual opening, not just the title.

Specialty, schedule, location, experience, availability, licensing, and client requirements are considered together before a candidate moves forward.

01

Recruit by specialty and market

Searches are shaped around the clinical discipline, geography, schedule, and availability needed for the opening.

02

Qualify beyond the resume

Experience, role fit, availability, work history, and required credentials are reviewed against the specific requirement.

03

Confirm the practical fit

Shift expectations, location, start timing, assignment length, and other role-specific details are addressed before submission.

04

Present against the requirement

Candidate information is organized around what the client or program needs to evaluate the submission efficiently.

Assignment Readiness

The work between selection and start matters.

Once a clinician is selected, the focus shifts to the requirements that make the assignment possible. Readiness varies by role, facility, state, and program.

01

License & certification

Applicable professional licenses, certifications, and role-specific requirements.

02

Clinical documentation

Required employment, skills, health, screening, and compliance documentation based on the assignment.

03

Client & program requirements

Facility-specific onboarding, forms, approvals, and program steps completed within the required workflow.

04

Start coordination

Final readiness, schedule, reporting instructions, and assignment details aligned before the clinician starts.
